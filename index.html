Ari Uploader

Ari Uploader is a private tool that uploads its owner's own finished videos to the owner's own YouTube channels, to the owner's own TikTok accounts, and to the owner's own Instagram accounts and Facebook Pages. It is not a public service: there is no sign-up, and only accounts the owner owns or administers are connected to it.

What it does

  • Uploads a video file, with its title, description, tags and privacy setting, to a YouTube channel the owner has connected. Every upload is approved by the owner first.
  • Reads the connected channel's name, handle and ID once, to confirm the right channel was connected.
  • Sends a video to a TikTok account the owner has connected through TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API, as a draft in the owner's TikTok inbox for the owner to finish and post in the TikTok app. It reads the connected account's display name once, to confirm the right account was connected.
  • Publishes a video as an Instagram Reel, or a Facebook Page Reel, to an Instagram Business or Creator account or Facebook Page the owner has connected, using Meta's Graph API. Every post is approved by the owner first. It reads the account's ID and username to confirm the right account was connected, and its latest posts to confirm a post went up.
