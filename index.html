Ari Uploader

Ari Uploader is a private tool that uploads its owner's own finished videos to the owner's own YouTube channels, and to the owner's own Instagram accounts and Facebook Pages. It is not a public service: there is no sign-up, and only accounts the owner owns or administers are connected to it.

What it does

  • Uploads a video file, with its title, description, tags and privacy setting, to a YouTube channel the owner has connected. Every upload is approved by the owner first.
  • Reads the connected channel's name, handle and ID once, to confirm the right channel was connected.
  • Publishes a video as an Instagram Reel, or a Facebook Page Reel, to an Instagram Business or Creator account or Facebook Page the owner has connected, using Meta's Graph API. Every post is approved by the owner first. It reads the account's ID and username to confirm the right account was connected, and its latest posts to confirm a post went up.

Ari Uploader uses the YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy and our privacy policy.

Posting to Instagram and Facebook follows the Meta Platform Terms; see the Instagram Privacy Policy and the Meta Privacy Policy.
